      Privacy

      Your most recent location and outfit choices are stored in this browser. Submitted city/ZIP searches are sent to Nominatim; location coordinates are sent to the National Weather Service for forecasts. Device coordinates are not sent to Nominatim. This app has no account or server storing your location. OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
